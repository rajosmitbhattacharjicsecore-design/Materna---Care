Pregnancy-related Asana Suggestions

Prenatal Yoga

Safety Disclaimer: General educational guidance only. Never force any posture. Discontinue immediately if you feel dizziness, nausea, or abdominal strain. Confirm suitability with your obstetrician.

Asana 1

🧘 Cat-Cow (Marjaryasana)

Move gently between arch and curl on hands and knees. Relieves lumbar pressure and encourages fetal anterior positioning.

Asana 2

🌿 Supported Child's Pose

Widen your knees to create plenty of space for your belly. Place a bolster or pillow under your chest for complete support.

Asana 3

✨ Bound Angle (Baddha Konasana)

Sit with soles together and knees out. Sit on an elevated cushion to relieve hip strain. Gently opens the pelvic basin.
